# Wanted Level

*Your wanted level online is always five stars. You just can't see it.*

A free guide that teaches kids online privacy through the wanted level from games like GTA, with a parent side for setting up the phone together. In English and French.

**Live site:** https://wantedlevelca.github.io

## What this site doesn't do

No tracking. No ads. No cookies. No accounts. No scripts, so the page can't run any code on your device. The tick boxes are plain checkboxes: nothing you tick leaves your screen, and it all disappears when you close the page.

## Don't trust us. Check us.

The whole site is three files: `index.html` (kid side), `parents.html` (parent side) and `style.css` (the look). Read them. Every change ever made is in this repository's history.

If any copy of this site asks for your name, email, login or an app download, it isn't us.

## Not a company. Not the government.

Made for free. We take no money. If it helped, give to [Kids Help Phone](https://kidshelpphone.ca).

**Found a mistake?** Tell us: wantedlevelca@proton.me

**Kids:** if something online is scaring you, text CONNECT to 686868 or call 1-800-668-6868. Free, any time.
